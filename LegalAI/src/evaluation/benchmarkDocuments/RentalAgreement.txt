RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement ("Lease") is made on January 15, 2026, by and between landlord Jane Smith ("Landlord") and tenant Robert Johnson ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord leases to the Tenant the residential property located at Apartment 4B, 123 Maple Street, Seattle, Washington 98101 (the "Premises"). The Premises shall be used solely as a private residential dwelling for the Tenant and their immediate family.

2. LEASE TERM
The term of this Lease shall be for a fixed period of twelve (12) months, commencing on February 1, 2026, and ending on January 31, 2027. Upon expiration of the lease term, this Lease shall automatically convert to a month-to-month tenancy unless either party provides sixty (60) days written notice of non-renewal.

3. RENT PAYMENT
The Tenant shall pay the Landlord a monthly rent of $2,200, due on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month. A late fee of $100 shall be charged if rent is not received by the fifth (5th) day of the month. Payment shall be made via electronic bank transfer to the Landlord's designated account.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon signing this Lease, the Tenant shall deposit with the Landlord the sum of $2,500 as security for any damage caused to the Premises during the lease term. The security deposit shall be held in an interest-bearing account and returned to the Tenant within twenty-one (21) days after termination of tenancy, minus any deductions for unpaid rent or cleaning/repair costs.

5. PET POLICY AND RULES
No pets, including dogs, cats, or birds, shall be kept on the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. If consent is granted, an additional pet deposit of $500 shall be paid. The Tenant agrees to keep the Premises clean and comply with all building regulations regarding noise, garbage disposal, and parking.

6. SUBLETTING
The Tenant shall not sublet the Premises, nor assign this Lease, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any unauthorized subletting or assignment shall be considered a material breach of this Lease and grounds for immediate eviction.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Washington. Any dispute arising under this Lease shall be resolved in the municipal or district courts of King County, Washington.
