INDUSTRIAL HEAVY EQUIPMENT LEASE CONTRACT

This Industrial Heavy Equipment Lease Contract ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. United Machinery Rental Corp ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. BuildFast Infrastructure Projects ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the lease and supply of three CAT-320 hydraulic excavator machines. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The lessee is responsible for all routine oil changes and track repairs.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: rental fee of $4,500 per machine per calendar month. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the fixed lease duration of eighteen (18) months. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing ten (10) days maintenance request notice to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Arizona. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Maricopa County, Phoenix.
