CIVIL PLAINT FOR RECOVERY OF OUTSTANDING BUSINESS DEBTS

This Civil Plaint for Recovery of Outstanding Business Debts ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Krishna Trading Agency (Plaintiff) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Radhe Textiles Limited (Defendant) ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the recovery of Rs. 8,50,000 due for supply of raw cotton materials fabric. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The defendant failed to clear unpaid invoices despite multiple reminders.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: claim of Rs. 8.5 Lakhs principal plus 18% compound interest per annum. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the active litigation civil recovery proceeding. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing thirty (30) days summons reply requirement to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Order 7 Rule 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC). Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Honorable City Civil Court, Hyderabad, Telangana.
