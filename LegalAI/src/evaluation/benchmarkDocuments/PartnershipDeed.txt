GENERAL BUSINESS PARTNERSHIP DEED

This General Business Partnership Deed ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. David Miller (Partner A) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Michael Chang (Partner B) ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the joint operation and asset management of the downtown bakery outlet storefront business. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. Initial capital investment of $50,000 is contributed equally by each partner.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: equal fifty percent profit distribution split after tax deductions. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the ten (10) years fixed duration. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing ninety (90) days dissolution notice requirement to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Illinois. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Cook County, Chicago.
