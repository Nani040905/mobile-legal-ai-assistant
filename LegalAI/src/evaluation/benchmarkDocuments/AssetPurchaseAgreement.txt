BUSINESS ASSET PURCHASE AND SALE CONTRACT

This Business Asset Purchase and Sale Contract ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Gourmet Foods Retailers ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Sunrise Bakeries Holding ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the conveyance of kitchen machinery, ovens, supply contracts, and recipes list inventory. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The seller agrees to a three-year non-compete within a fifty-mile radius.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: total purchase transaction cost of $350,000 paid via wire transfer. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the closed immediately at the completion date. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing five (5) business days escrow release notice to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Ohio. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Franklin County, Columbus.
