REGISTERED GIFT DEED FOR RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY

This Registered Gift Deed for Residential Property ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Ramesh Chawla (Donor Father) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Sanjay Chawla (Donee Son) ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the unconditional transfer of residential house located at 12 Mall Road, Shimla. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The stamp duty has been paid in full based on the local circle rates.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: transfer made out of natural love and affection without cash consideration. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the irrevocable permanent property conveyance. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing immediate registration at sub-registrar office to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Section 122 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Office of the Sub-Registrar, Shimla, Himachal Pradesh.
