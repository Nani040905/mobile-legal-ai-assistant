DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (DPA) GDPR ANNEX

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) GDPR Annex ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. EuroCloud Storage ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. German Health Systems AG ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the handling and secure cloud storage of European Union patient biometric details. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The processor must notify the controller of sub-processor changes within 10 days.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: included inside the master platform services fees schedule. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the co-terminus with the primary cloud storage agreement. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing forty-eight (48) hours data breach notice to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Federal Republic of Germany. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Frankfurt District Court.
