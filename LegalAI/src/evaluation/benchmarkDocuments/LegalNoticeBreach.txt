LEGAL NOTICE FOR BREACH OF BUSINESS CONTRACT

This Legal Notice for Breach of Business Contract ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Advocate Anil Mishra on behalf of clients ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Metro Real Estate Developers Limited ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the demand to cure construction delays and hand over flat possession within 30 days. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. Failure to reply within 15 days will result in immediate civil court filing.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: demand for refund of booking amount of Rs. 15 Lakhs with interest. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the active warning period before lawsuit filing. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing fifteen (15) days statutory reply window to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Section 80 of the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC). Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in District Consumer Commission, Pune, Maharashtra.
