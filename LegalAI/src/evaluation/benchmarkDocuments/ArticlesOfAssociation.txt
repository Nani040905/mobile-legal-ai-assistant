ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION OF PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANY

This Articles of Association of Private Limited Company ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Registrar of Joint Stock Companies ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Apex Logistics Private Limited ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the internal governance rules for share allocation, director borrowing powers, and auditing audits. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The maximum number of shareholders is capped at two hundred members.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: authorized share capital of Rs. 10 Lakhs split into 1 Lakh shares. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the perpetual corporate existence until winding up. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing twenty-one (21) days annual general meeting notice to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Republic of India. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) Delhi Bench.
