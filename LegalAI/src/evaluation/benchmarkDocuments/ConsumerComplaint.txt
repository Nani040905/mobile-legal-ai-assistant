CONSUMER COMPLAINT AFFIDAVIT

Before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Bangalore, Karnataka.

COMPLAINANT:
Mr. Suresh Kumar, residing at Flat 102, Green Meadows, Bangalore 560037.

OPPOSITE PARTY:
ElectroMax Retailers Ltd., registered office at 45 MG Road, Bangalore 560001.

1. STATEMENT OF FACTS
On November 10, 2025, the Complainant purchased a premium smart refrigerator model EM-990 from ElectroMax Retailers Ltd. for a total sum of Rs. 85,000. A copy of the purchase invoice is attached as Annexure A. The product came with a comprehensive two-year manufacturer warranty.

2. DEFECT AND CORRESPONDENCE
Within three weeks of installation, the refrigerator's cooling compressor failed completely, causing food spoilage and significant inconvenience. The Complainant registered a formal complaint with the Opposite Party's customer support on December 2, 2025. Despite five follow-up emails and three service visits by technicians, the defect was not repaired, and the retailer refused to replace the refrigerator or issue a refund.

3. WARRANTY BREACH
The refusal of ElectroMax Retailers Ltd. to repair the defective compressor or replace the product under the active two-year comprehensive warranty constitutes a clear deficiency of service and unfair trade practice under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

4. PRAYER AND COMPENSATION
The Complainant prays that this Commission direct the Opposite Party to:
(a) Refund the full purchase price of Rs. 85,000 with 12% interest per annum;
(b) Pay Rs. 20,000 as compensation for mental harassment and food spoilage; and
(c) Pay Rs. 5,000 towards litigation costs.
