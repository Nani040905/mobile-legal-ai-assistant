AFFIDAVIT IN SUPPORT OF FIRE INSURANCE CLAIM

This Affidavit in Support of Fire Insurance Claim ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Harsimran Singh (Policyholder) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. National Insurance Company Limited ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the statement of inventory lost during warehouse fire incident on April 15, 2026. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The fire survey report confirms electrical short circuit as the root cause.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: claim value of Rs. 35,00,000 for destroyed cotton stocks. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the active claim processing assessment phase. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing seven (7) days notice of fire incident served to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Insurance Act, 1938. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC), Delhi.
