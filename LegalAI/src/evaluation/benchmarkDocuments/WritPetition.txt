WRIT PETITION UNDER ARTICLE 226 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA

This Writ Petition under Article 226 of the Constitution of India ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Resident Welfare Association Sector 5 ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. State of Uttar Pradesh & Municipal Board ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the plea to issue Writ of Mandamus directing cleanup of illegal garbage dump yard. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The dump yard violates Article 21 rights to a clean and healthy environment.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: nominal court fee of Rs. 250 paid at filing registry. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the active litigation until final order/judgment. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing fourteen (14) days notice to opposite government parties to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Article 226 of the Constitution of India. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Honorable High Court of Judicature at Allahabad.
