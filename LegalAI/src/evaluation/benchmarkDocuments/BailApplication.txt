BAIL APPLICATION UNDER SECTION 439 OF THE CRPC / SECTION 482 OF THE BNSS

This Bail Application under Section 439 of the CrPC / Section 482 of the BNSS ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Sunil Gupta (Accused Applicant) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. State (NCT of Delhi) Prosecution ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the request for grant of regular bail in connection with FIR 054/2026. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The applicant is a respectable businessman with no prior criminal records.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: personal surety bond of Rs. 50,000 with one local solvent surety. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the valid during the pendency of the criminal trial. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing three (3) days notice to the public prosecutor to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Court of the Sessions Judge, Patiala House Courts, New Delhi.
