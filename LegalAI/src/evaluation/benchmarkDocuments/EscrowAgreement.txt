TRIPARTITE ESCROW SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Tripartite Escrow Services Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. SafeGuard Escrow Trust Ltd ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Buyer Group & Seller Group Co-Parties ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the holding and release of transaction funds pending software code delivery confirmation. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The escrow agent is a passive depository only and has no fiduciary duties.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: flat escrow administration fee of $2,000 split between buyer and seller. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the terminated upon successful disbursement of all escrow funds. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing five (5) days dispute filing window to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Nevada. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Clark County, Las Vegas.
