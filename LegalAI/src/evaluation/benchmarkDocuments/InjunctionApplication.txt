APPLICATION FOR TEMPORARY INJUNCTION UNDER ORDER 39 RULES 1 & 2 OF CPC

This Application for Temporary Injunction under Order 39 Rules 1 & 2 of CPC ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Harish Mehta (Plaintiff Owner) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Suresh Patel (Defendant Encroacher) ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the plea to restrain defendant from constructing a wall on the agricultural boundary line. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The plaintiff has a strong prima facie case and balance of convenience is in his favor.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: standard civil court fees paid as per the Court Fees Act. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the valid until final disposal of the main property suit. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing immediate ad-interim ex-parte hearing request to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Order 39 of the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC). Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Senior Civil Judge, Vadodara Court, Gujarat.
