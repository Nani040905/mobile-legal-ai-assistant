SECURED COMMERCIAL LOAN AND FINANCE AGREEMENT

This Secured Commercial Loan and Finance Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Evergreen Savings Bank ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Apex Warehouse Facilities LLC ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the advance of five hundred thousand dollars principal loan amount. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The loan is secured by a first lien on the warehouse property asset.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: fixed annual interest rate of 7.25% payable monthly. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the maturity term period of sixty (60) months total. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing three (3) days event of default notice to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Colorado. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Denver County Court, Denver.
