UNSECURED PROMISSORY NOTE AGREEMENT

This Unsecured Promissory Note Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Charles Henderson (Borrower) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Margaret Vance (Lender) ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the written promise to pay thirty thousand dollars for school tuition fees. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. Prepayment without penalty is permitted at any time during the term.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: accrued simple interest rate of 4% per year paid in full at maturity. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the payable in a single lump sum on December 31, 2027. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing five (5) days default cure period to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Utah. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Salt Lake County, Salt Lake City.
