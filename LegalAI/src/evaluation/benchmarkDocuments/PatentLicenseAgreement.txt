EXCLUSIVE PATENT LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Exclusive Patent License Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. BioGen Research Institute ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. AstraPharma Enterprises ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the use of the patented high-speed chemical synthesis technology described in Patent US-99281. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The license includes all child continuation-in-part patent claims.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: running royalty fee of 3.5% of all net commercial product sales. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the until the expiration date of the patent rights. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing ninety (90) days notice of patent breach to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Massachusetts. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Suffolk County, Boston.
