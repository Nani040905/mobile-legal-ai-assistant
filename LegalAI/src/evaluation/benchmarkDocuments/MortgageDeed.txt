REAL ESTATE MORTGAGE AND TRUST DEED

This Real Estate Mortgage and Trust Deed ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. First Mutual Lending LLC ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Thomas Brown (Property Owner) ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the pledge of the real estate residential property located at 54 Oak Lane. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. Failure to pay property taxes counts as a material default under the mortgage.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: monthly principal and interest payment of $1,750. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the thirty (30) years amortization schedule. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing thirty (30) days acceleration and foreclosure notice to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of North Carolina. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Wake County, Raleigh.
