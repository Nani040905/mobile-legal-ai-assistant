POLICE CHARGE SHEET

Under Section 173 of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) / Section 193 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS).

In the Court of the Metropolitan Magistrate, Saket Courts, New Delhi.

State vs. Rajesh Yadav (Accused)
Police Station: Saket. FIR No: 0089/2026. Date of Filing: June 1, 2026.

1. ACCUSED DETAILS
Name: Rajesh Yadav, Age: 32, son of Ram Yadav, residing at Flat 301, Block B, Sangam Vihar, New Delhi. The accused is currently in judicial custody.

2. STATEMENT OF OFFENSE
The accused, Rajesh Yadav, was arrested on May 10, 2026, from Saket Community Center. He is charged with knowingly purchasing and attempting to resell stolen electronic items, specifically an Apple iPad Pro belonging to Ramesh Patel, which was stolen on May 4, 2026.

3. PHYSICAL AND DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE
The prosecution presents the following evidence:
(a) Recovery Memo: The stolen Apple iPad Pro (Serial Number: IPAD12345) was recovered from the physical possession of the accused at the time of arrest;
(b) Call Detail Records (CDR) linking the accused with the prime theft suspect; and
(c) CCTV footage from Saket Community Center showing the accused handling the stolen iPad.

4. SECTIONS OF LAW CHARGED
The accused is charged under the following sections:
(a) Section 411 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) / Section 317 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) for dishonestly receiving stolen property; and
(b) Section 120B of the IPC / Section 61 of the BNS for criminal conspiracy.

5. WITNESSES
The prosecution proposes to examine five witnesses, including the complainant Ramesh Patel, Sub-Inspector Anil Sharma, and security guard Mohan Lal.
