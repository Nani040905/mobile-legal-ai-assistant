TRADEMARK ASSIGNMENT AND TRANSFER DEED

This Trademark Assignment and Transfer Deed ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Legacy Brands Corp ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Modern Goods Holding Inc ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the assignment of the registered logo trademark registration number TR-4429. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The assignment transfers all goodwill and international registration extensions.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: total purchase consideration of $75,000 paid at execution. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the unconditional permanent transfer. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing ten (10) days notice to register the transfer to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of New York. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in New York County, Manhattan.
