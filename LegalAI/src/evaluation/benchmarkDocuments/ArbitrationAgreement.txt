STANDALONE DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND ARBITRATION AGREEMENT

This Standalone Dispute Resolution and Arbitration Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Omega FinServ Corp ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Apex Wealth Investors ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the resolution of all future financial disputes through binding private arbitration. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The arbitration panel shall consist of three retired federal judges.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: arbitrator fees split equally between the contesting parties. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the survives expiration or termination of the master agreement. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing thirty (30) days notice to initiate arbitration to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Federal Arbitration Act (FAA). Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in American Arbitration Association (AAA) in New York.
