FAST FOOD OUTLET FRANCHISE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Fast Food Outlet Franchise License Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Burger Palace Franchising Ltd ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Local Eats Franchisee Inc ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the operation of a standardized quick service restaurant and proprietary brand menu access. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The franchisee must purchase all secret sauces and breads from the franchisor.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: ongoing royalty commission of 6% of weekly gross sales figures. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the fixed duration of ten (10) years with renewal options. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing thirty (30) days default remedy notice to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the State of Georgia. Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Fulton County, Atlanta.
