WRITTEN STATEMENT OF DEFENSE BY DEFENDANT

This Written Statement of Defense by Defendant ("Agreement") is executed on this day, by and between:
1. Radhe Textiles Limited (Defendant) ("First Party" / "Disclosing Party" / "Grantor"), and
2. Krishna Trading Agency (Plaintiff) ("Second Party" / "Receiving Party" / "Grantee").

1. SCOPE AND CORE OBLIGATIONS
The primary scope of work and services governed by this agreement includes the para-wise denial of plaint allegations and statement that goods delivered were defective. The parties agree to perform their respective roles with due diligence and in accordance with standard industry practices. The plaintiff delivered cotton with moisture content exceeding acceptable limits.

2. CONSIDERATION AND PAYMENT TERMS
In consideration of the rights and services granted herein, the parties agree to the payment schedule consisting of: counter-claim for refund of shipping charges of Rs. 50,000. Payment shall be disbursed in full compliance with the invoices submitted. All invoices must be cleared in accordance with the billing cycle terms.

3. DURATION AND TERMINATION
The contract term shall remain in force for the pending trial adjudication stage. Either party may terminate this agreement early by providing thirty (30) days filing deadline extension requests to the other party. Upon termination, all outstanding payments must be cleared immediately.

4. GOVERNING LAW AND COURT JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted in accordance with, the Order 8 Rule 1 of the Code of Civil Procedure (CPC). Any disputes, differences, or legal actions arising out of or related to this agreement shall be brought exclusively before the courts located in Honorable City Civil Court, Hyderabad, Telangana.
